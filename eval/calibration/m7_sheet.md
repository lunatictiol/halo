## f04 · faithfulness  (abstractive, text-image)

**Question:** How do link functions like logit and probit relate to generalized linear models?

**Answer:** Link functions like logit and probit are integral to generalized linear models (GLMs) as they connect the linear predictor to the mean of the distribution function [2410.14871v2 §4 p.12]. The logit function, for instance, is used in GLMs to model binary outcomes by transforming probabilities into log-odds [2410.13100v1 §2 p.5]. Similarly, the probit function, which is the inverse of the standard normal cumulative distribution function, is also used in GLMs for similar purposes [2408.06977v4 §2 p.6]. Thus, these link functions enable the modeling of non-linear relationships within the framework of GLMs [2410.14871v2 §4 p.12] [2410.13100v1 §2 p.5] [2408.06977v4 §2 p.6].
